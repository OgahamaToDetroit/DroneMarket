# Source notes — Thailand drone market sizing, 2025

## Market definition

The broad market includes new drone platforms sold for use in Thailand, payloads and sensors, accessories, software, local integration, maintenance and training, and drone-enabled operating/data services. It excludes counter-drone systems, used-equipment resale, and goods imported only for re-export.

## Facts used

- Customs HS 8806 imports in 2025: THB 6.804bn. This is a trade-flow value, not domestic market size.
- Customs demand that passes the project gate: THB 1.677bn. The project's rule-based adjusted floor is THB 1.830bn.
- New NBTC registrations in 2025: 43,129; 37,546 in 2024; 22,476 in 2022.
- e-GP drone awards in FY2025: THB 871.1m. Identifiable drone and aerial-survey service awards: approximately THB 255.1m.
- Research benchmarks: IMARC USD 212.9m; Data Bridge USD 197.43m in 2024 and 14.1% forecast growth (about USD 225.3m implied for 2025); Next Move Strategy Consulting USD 261.9m and 45.24k units; Grand View Research USD 737.7m.
- Currency conversion for comparison: THB 33.33 per USD, implied by the project's 2025 customs USD and THB totals.

## Scenario formulas

`platform end-market = THB 1.830bn × channel factor`

`payloads/accessories/software/integration = platform end-market × add-on ratio`

`services = THB 0.255bn public service floor × (1 + private-service multiple)`

`broad market = platform end-market + add-ons/software/integration + services`

The scenario assumptions are judgments, not observed facts. The base case uses a 1.35 channel factor, add-ons/software equal to 65% of platform revenue, and private services equal to three times the visible public-service floor.

## Synthesis

- Bottom-up scenarios: THB 3.6bn / 5.1bn / 7.0bn.
- External-report cluster excluding the Grand View outlier: about THB 7.1bn–8.7bn, median about THB 7.5bn.
- A 60% weight on the direct local base case and 40% on the external-report median gives about THB 6.1bn.
- Decision range used in the report: THB 5.0bn–7.5bn, with a working midpoint of about THB 6.0bn.

## Validation notes

- The Next Move 2025 unit forecast of 45.24k is close to the project's 43,129 registrations, a useful volume cross-check.
- Grand View's USD 737.7m estimate implies about THB 24.6bn and is treated as an outlier because it is 3–5 times the local-evidence model and other research estimates.
- e-GP is not added to hardware imports because public hardware contracts may already be embodied in imported platforms; only its service portion anchors the services floor.
- The estimate should be shared as a range. The largest uncertainty is private service revenue and the value of payloads, sensors, software, and integration classified outside HS 8806.
